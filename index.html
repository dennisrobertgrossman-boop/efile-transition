SIX NEURAL STRESS TESTS

TEST 01

GLITCH//GRID

THE ARROWS GET REWIRED

Steer a runner across the grid, collect data shards and grow your trail. Every 30–45 seconds each arrow starts steering in a different direction.

PLAY TEST 02

CHROMA//LIE

THE RULE GETS REWRITTEN

A colour word flashes up in the wrong ink. Hit the right colour button before time runs out — but the rule for which colour is "right" keeps changing, and the buttons swap places.

PLAY TEST 03

VERTIGO//DRIFT

THE WORLD GETS ROTATED

Glide around an arena, grab shards and dodge hunter drones. Every 30–45 seconds the whole arena rotates or mirrors, while your arrows stay wired to the old world.

PLAY TEST 04

ECHO//LOCK

THE ANSWER GETS TWISTED

A memory test. Watch a sequence of arrows, then enter it — in the same order, backwards, or with every arrow flipped, depending on a rule that keeps changing.

PLAY TEST 05

PURGE//PROTOCOL

THE TARGET GETS REDEFINED

An attention test. Tap the nodes that match the purge order and leave the decoys alone. The order switches between colours, shapes and "everything except…".

PLAY TEST 06

SYNC//PULSE

THE RHYTHM GETS DISRUPTED

A one-button timing test. Tap when the orbiting marker is inside the zone. Then the orbit reverses, speeds up, goes dark, or the zone starts to slide.
